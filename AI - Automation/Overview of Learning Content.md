# Overview of Learning Content

## Introduction

This section focuses on applying the concepts and knowledge gained from learning n8n, workflow automation, and artificial intelligence to practical, real-world scenarios.

Rather than focusing only on theory, the goal is to build functional automation workflows that solve everyday business and operational problems.

The projects will explore how different applications, systems, APIs, and AI tools can work together to automate repetitive tasks, improve efficiency, support decision-making, and simplify business processes.

## Learning Objectives

Through these practical projects, I aim to:

- Understand how to translate business problems into automation solutions.
- Build workflows using n8n and connect different applications.
- Understand how data moves between workflow nodes and applications.
- Apply triggers, actions, conditions, and data transformations.
- Integrate AI models into workflows where intelligent processing is required.
- Understand how to test, troubleshoot, and improve automation workflows.
- Apply automation to Project Management and other business operations.
- Document each project, including its purpose, design, implementation, and results.

## Project Categories

The practical projects will cover different areas of business and technology:

### 1. Traditional Workflow Automation

Projects focused on automating repetitive tasks using predefined rules and conditions, without necessarily requiring AI.

### 2. AI-Powered Workflow Automation

Projects that integrate AI models into workflows to perform tasks such as content generation, information extraction, classification, and summarisation.

### 3. Business Process Automation

Projects that demonstrate how automation can improve business operations across areas such as customer service, recruitment, marketing, administration, and sales.

### 4. Project Management Automation

Projects focused on improving project delivery and coordination through automated task management, status updates

Expected Outcome

By the end of this learning journey, I aim to have a portfolio of practical automation projects that demonstrate my understanding of workflow automation, n8n, AI integration, and the application of these technologies to real business problems.

The focus is not simply on building workflows, but on understanding the problems they solve, the value they create, and how they can be managed effectively from a Project Management and business perspective.
